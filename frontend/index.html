Subsystem breakdown

1. Vanilla RAG

Retrieves initial vector matches and pipes directly into the generator. Zero-shot execution.

2. Self-Critique RAG

Appends an LLM-as-a-judge layer to categorize the output as SUPPORTED, UNSUPPORTED, or UNCERTAIN against the evidence.

3. Knowledge-Repair RAG

Intercepts UNSUPPORTED or UNCERTAIN generations. Dispatches secondary context retrieval and loops back for final resolution.
